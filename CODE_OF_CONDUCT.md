# Code of conduct

DisplaySwitch contributors are expected to keep project spaces welcoming, constructive, and focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful when discussing code, hardware, documentation, and differing experience levels.
- Give actionable technical feedback and assume good intent.
- Respect privacy. Never publish another person's pairing code, network details, device identifiers, logs, or private correspondence.
- Credit contributors and third-party projects accurately.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate disclosure of private information, spam, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions, or other project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security reporting channel described in `SECURITY.md`; do not open a public issue containing sensitive details.
